1. Who we are

"SmartMedi", "we" and "us" refer to Dr. G. Sree Hari Reddy Gadekallu, an individual developer based in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh, India. SmartMedi is the name under which he publishes apps. On the App Store the apps are listed under "Sree Hari Reddy Gadekallu". For the purposes of data protection law, he is the party responsible for these apps (the "data fiduciary" or "controller").

2. Which apps this covers

This policy covers every app we publish on the Apple App Store and Google Play, now or in the future, unless a particular app links to its own separate policy. The app-by-app summary at the end lists which features each app uses. Where an app's App Store privacy label or Google Play Data safety section gives more detail for that app, read it together with this policy.

3. What we don't collect

We don't run our own servers for these apps, so we don't receive or store your personal information. Specifically, we don't collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any patient or health records. Our apps don't require sign-up or log-in. If a future app ever offers an account, this policy will be updated before that app is released. That update will explain what the account stores and how to delete it from inside the app or by email.

4. Data kept on your device

Apps may save things like your settings, quiz progress, bookmarks, scores and unlocked lessons. This is stored locally on your device so the app remembers where you left off. On Apple devices it may be included in your iCloud or device backups, under your own Apple account settings. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

5. Advertising

Some free apps, or the free versions of apps, display ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob and its partners may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), if you allow it
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • device type, operating system, language and app version
  • ad interactions, such as views and taps

On iOS, we only use your advertising identifier if you choose "Allow" on Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised using that identifier. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice in the app, where available, or in your device settings. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Apps that carry no ads don't include the AdMob code at all.

6. In-app purchases

Some apps offer paid upgrades, content unlocks or subscriptions. All payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play Billing). We never see or store your card or bank details. The store tells the app only that a purchase was made, so the app can unlock what you bought and let you restore it on another device. Purchase records, refunds and subscription management are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies: Apple, Google. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

7. Analytics & crash reports

To find bugs and see which features are used, some apps may use Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics). These tools collect a random app-instance identifier, device model, OS version, app version, crash traces and general usage events, such as which screen was opened. They don't collect your name or anything you type. We see this information only in aggregate. Apple may also share anonymous crash and usage statistics with us, but only if you have turned on "Share with App Developers" in your iPhone's settings.

8. Health information

Our medical apps are educational and reference tools for students and healthcare professionals. Values you enter into a calculator are processed on your device and are not sent to us or to anyone else. Please don't enter information that identifies a patient. The apps don't provide medical advice or diagnosis and are not a substitute for clinical judgement.

9. Children

Our medical apps are intended for adults and students aged 13 and over. They are not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Apps made specifically for children follow additional rules. This means any app we list in Apple's Kids category or in Google Play's "Designed for Families" programme. Such an app collects no personal information, shows no behavioural or personalised ads, uses no third-party tracking, and puts links out of the app and purchases behind a parental gate. These apps are designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the GDPR, and Apple's and Google's policies for children's apps. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your choices & controls

  • Ad tracking on iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) → Delete advertising ID.
  • Remove ads: where offered, a paid upgrade removes ads.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. This includes rights under India's DPDP Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and California's CCPA/CPRA. Because we don't hold personal data on our own systems, most requests are best handled through the controls above or directly with Google or Apple. We are still happy to help: write to us and we will respond within 30 days. We don't sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under California law, apart from the advertising described in section 5, which you can switch off.

12. Retention, security & international transfers

We don't keep personal data on our own systems. Data collected by Google or Apple is kept and protected according to their policies and may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Any emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a change materially affects how an app handles your data, we will make it clear in the app or its store listing before the change takes effect.

Support

For every SmartMedi app on iPhone, iPad and Android

Need help? Email sreehari.kmc@gmail.com. Please include the app name, your device and OS version (for example iPhone 13, iOS 18), and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot. You'll usually get a reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

I bought an upgrade. How do I get it on a new device?

Open the app and tap Restore Purchases (usually in Settings or on the upgrade screen). Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds go through them. On iOS, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use your Google Play order history. We can't issue refunds directly, but we're happy to help you sort out the problem first.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Can I remove the ads?

Where an app shows ads, there is a paid upgrade or Pro version without them. You can also limit ad personalisation in your device settings. See the privacy policy.

I found a medical or factual error.

Thank you. Please email the app name, the screen or card, and what you think is wrong, with a reference if you have one. Corrections are made in the next update.

Where are the Android versions?

The Android apps are temporarily unavailable while the Google Play developer account completes verification. They will be back on Google Play soon.

Do the apps collect my data?

No account is needed, and anything you type stays on your device. Ads and purchases are handled by Google and Apple. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Can I use these apps to make clinical decisions?

They are learning and quick-reference tools. Always check against current guidelines and use your own clinical judgement. They are not medical devices and do not diagnose or treat patients.
